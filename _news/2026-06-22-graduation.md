This June, three master’s students from our group are graduating: Ms. Yuting Cheng, Ms. Shuyan Xiao, and Mr. Dexin Zhang. Congratulations to them on the successful completion of their master’s degrees, and we sincerely appreciate their valuable contributions to the group throughout their studies. We wish them continued success in their future endeavors and look forward to seeing their achievements in the next stages of their careers.

They are graduating with the following master’s theses:
- Ms. Yuting Cheng, *Joint Optimization of Cross-Line Assembling and Scheduling for Modular Buses*
- Ms. Shuyan Xiao, *On the Design and Operations of En-route Express Services for Modular Autonomous Transit Systems*
- Mr. Dexin Zhang, *Inference of Electric Vehicle Charging Decision-Making Behavior and Charging Demand Under Partially Observed Data*
